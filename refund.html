Refund Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

1. 3-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We stand behind our service. If you face technical issues or are unsatisfied with the translation quality, you may request a full refund within 3 days of your initial purchase, provided that you have not exhausted your allocated translation minutes.

After 3 days, or if the allocated minutes have been fully used, all payments are strictly non-refundable.

2. Refund Processing Time

Once your refund request is approved, it is processed immediately on our end. However, please note that it may take an additional 3-5 working days for the funds to reflect in your bank account or credit card balance, depending entirely on your bank or payment provider.

3. Exceptions for Refund

We may also consider a refund request under the following exceptional circumstances (even beyond the usual terms):

  • If you were charged multiple times due to a technical error on our end.
  • If the service was completely inaccessible to you for a prolonged period due to our server downtime.

4. Cancellation

You can cancel your subscription at any time to prevent future billing. Your account will remain active until the end of the current billing cycle. No partial refunds will be issued for unused time in the billing cycle.

5. How to Request a Refund

If you believe you are eligible for a refund under this 3-Day policy, please contact our support team at info@transvoice.ai with the email address used for your account. We review requests promptly.
